Founder

THE VISIONARY

A Legacy Built Since 1990.

Year 1990, the late Mohinuddin Patel began his journey in construction with a simple belief: build honestly, build to last, and let the work speak for itself. Over three decades, that principle earned him the trust of families across Mumbai and the Thane region. Today, that legacy lives on through his son, Azhar M. Patel, founder of Arkon Buildtech. Raised on his father’s values and shaped by years in the industry, Azhar combines that foundation of integrity with modern construction methods and a clear focus on quality, transparency, and timely delivery. At Arkon Buildtech, we specialise in residential projects, creating homes where families build their lives. Every project carries forward the standards set in 1990, and the promise that every home is built with the same care we would give our own.


A father's foundation. A son's vision. Homes built to last.


"We don't just build structures. We craft the spaces where life's most important moments unfold."
